## Introduction

<span style="color:#D0D0D0; font-size: 0.9em">
Olist is one of Brazil’s leading e-commerce companies. Founded in 2015, it connects small and medium-sized businesses to major online marketplaces across the country and is now expanding internationally. <br><br>
This project uses real commercial data from Olist (2016–2018), containing over 100,000 orders, to address the following questions:
</span>

<ul style="color:#D0D0D0; font-size: 0.9em; margin-top: 0.5em;">
  <li>How did Olist’s total sales evolve between 2016 and 2018?</li>
  <li>Which categories and customer groups should the company focus on to maximize growth?</li>
  <li>What operational improvements could reduce delays and increase satisfaction?</li>
</ul>
