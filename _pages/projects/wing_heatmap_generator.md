  Wing Damage Heatmap Generator
</div>


A custom R pipeline that generates spatial heatmaps of wing damage from insect wing image data to reveal damage frequency and positional trends.


## Motivation

In studies of insect flight performance and wing durability, understanding where and how frequently damage occurs can provide insight into evolutionary or biomechanical constraints. However, visualizing and quantifying this damage spatially is non-trivial.


## What I Did

Clearly describe what you built or analyzed.

Developed an image-processing pipeline in R.

Extracted and aggregated wing damage data from annotated image files.

Mapped the frequency of damage occurrences across the wing.

Generated high-resolution heatmaps to visualize spatial trends.

Packaged the workflow for reproducibility.
